# Hi, I'm Sailesh

I'm a QA Automation Engineer based in Chennai, India, though I spent the last few years testing software (and a fair bit of winter) in London, Ontario. My work sits at the intersection of catching things that break and building systems that stop them from breaking again — mostly for web and API applications, mostly with Playwright, Selenium, and a growing habit of getting AI tools to do the boring parts of testing for me.

## What I actually do

For about three years I was the QA person a small team leaned on to know whether a release was actually safe to ship. That meant writing test plans, digging into why a bug happened rather than just filing it, and building automation frameworks that could survive a fast-moving product without falling apart every sprint. One of the more satisfying things I built was a self-healing locator system — instead of a test breaking every time a developer renamed a button, it tries a few fallback strategies (and occasionally asks an AI model to figure out where the element went) before giving up.

I like the parts of QA that people don't usually put on a resume: the moment a flaky test finally stops being flaky, the small process fix that quietly saves everyone an hour a week, mentoring someone through their first real bug report.

## Outside of work

I'm happiest when I'm learning something I didn't know I needed to know, or when I'm somewhere new figuring out how a city actually works rather than just visiting it. I've lived and studied on two continents now, and that's given me a genuine appetite for travel that isn't going away. When I'm not out exploring, I'm usually deep into a story-driven game — the kind you remember for the narrative long after you've forgotten the mechanics.

## Where I'm headed

I'm currently open to QA Automation and SDET roles — anywhere I can keep building test systems that are genuinely reliable rather than just technically automated. If that's something you're hiring for, or just want to talk shop about testing, feel free to reach out.
